Triggers monitored in the express stream are listed on the twiki
During the 2015 data-taking, a variety of MET trigger algorithms was being tested. The most frequently used
algorithm was the cell algorithm, with lowest unprescaled trigger of 70 GeV, known as xe70. Other
algorithms under test included a topo-cluster-based algorithm, xe70_tc_lcw and two variations of that
algorithm with pileup mitigation https://cds.cern.ch/record/2140103/. Analyses which compared the
performance of this algorithm to the topo-cluster-based algorithm found that the topo-cluster based algorithm
had a sharper efficiency-turn-on as a function of offline MET, therefore higher efficiency at the same rate.
