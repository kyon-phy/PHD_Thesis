Trigger
Group
Single jet Multi jets MET HT
(a) Analyses using MET triggers should also check events that fire
HLT_noalg_L1J400 to see if any of these events were not fired by the met
trigger in the analysis phase-space.
(*) HLT_xe100_mht_L1XE50 ran unprescaled during D4-F1 other than at the
start of some high-luminosity runs.
The menu evolution for the un-prescaled triggers can also be found at
LowestUnprescaledMET.
Triggers monitored in the express stream are listed on the twiki
The lowest unprescaled trigger for 2016 uses the MHT algorithm. MHT is
the negative vector sum of trigger jet pT, where the trigger jet
calibration is EM + pileup subtraction + JES.
Luminosity calculation
https://atlas-tagservices.cern.ch/tagservices/RunBrowser/runBrowserReport/runBrowserReport.php?fnt=&pn=&runs=&gfile=data16_13TeV.periodAllYear_DetStatus-v84-pro20-16_DQDefects-00-02-04_PHYS_StandardGRL_All_Good.xml&smk=&cn=HLT_xe110_mht_L1XE50&stt=&stm=
The decision was made, based on a wide sample of physics analyses using
2015 data, to dedicate the majority of the rate to the topocluster-based
MET algorithms (tc_lcw and mht), calibrated at using the LCW algorithm,
rather than the cell algorithm. The MET-only trigger is being allocated
most rate rather than MET+X in order to maintain the lowest unprescaled
threshold possible, since the MET-only trigger is used by a very wide
range of analyses.
The menu choices were based on the findings from 2015 data. Some basic
performance plots from that data can be found in the 2015 Trigger
performance paper https://cds.cern.ch/record/2140103/ . More of the
rationale may be found in the Jira thread
https://its.cern.ch/jira/browse/ATR-13380 .
More general information on the content of the menu can be found at:
TriggerPhysicsMenu#Physics_menu_for_2016 and on the spreadsheets from the
Signature Coordination Meeting https://indico.cern.ch/event/512378/
