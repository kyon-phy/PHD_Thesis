Conclusion
• 0tau0b as CR (favoured)
• ~2sigma excess in VR, but seems like statistical fluctuation (we made a careful study)
• ad-hoc 20% uncertainty on W+HF/LF ratio
• post fit W+jets normalization factor 0.89+/-0.08
• 0tau1b as CR (not favored)
• no need to consider W+HF/LF ratio uncertainty
• post fit W+jets normalization factor 1.91+/-0.52 (how to interpret ~2sigma deviation from SM? we know data is 
likely fluctuated)
• 5~10% sensitivity loss
• Difficult to define the VR. 0tau0b and 1tau0b regions are not useful
• both 0tau0b and 0tau1b in the fit, to give a constraint on W+HF/LF ratio uncertainty ( fine to use, but a 
bit aggressive, favoured by the conveners?)
• post fit W+jets normalization factor 0.91+/-0.08
• 1sigma post-fit pull observed for W+HF/LF ratio nuisance parameter. Post fit uncertainty on that nuisance parameter 
is ~0.9 (poor stats in 0tau1b, so not so strong constraint but a bit aggressive comparing to the first method)
• no excess observed in all VRs/CRs
• Sensitivity is comparable to the first method. 
• WVR1tau could be used for the 0b ->1b extrapolation validation?
