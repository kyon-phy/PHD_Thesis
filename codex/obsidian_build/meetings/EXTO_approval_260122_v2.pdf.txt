### PAGE 14
Theory uncertainty estimation
• Theory uncertaintiesfor W+jets and top background are estimated, by following PMG
recommendations.
• Exception: the fraction of b/c jets in W+jets affects our analysis, that is, the fraction of b/c jets could be
the source of the W+jets modeling uncertainties when extrapolating the fit results from b-veto CR to
the b-tag SR/VRs.
• We tested with 10%, 20% and 30% uncertainty on W+jets. The sensitivity is not changed so much for
using 20% or 30% (given it is dominated by data stat uncertainty) and 20% is applied in the end.
• The uncertainty of “transferfactor (CR→SR)” is estimated.
• Most of uncertaintiesare cancelled out between SR/VR and CRand found to be less than 20%.
• CR definition is good, and we can use the phase space very similar to SR/VR.
• Uncertaintiesaffected by poor MC statistics are evaluatedwith looser selection cuts and /or
smoothing of distribution.
• 𝑍 → 𝜈𝜈 + 𝜏(fake) is a minor source of background. The MC modeling for fake tau is not perfect, so
we assigned 50% uncertainty on that since we observed disagreement~ 50% between data and
MC in 𝑍 → 𝑙𝑙 + 𝜏(fake) VR.
• Diboson and Z+jets are assigned with 30% mis-modeling uncertainty.(The impact on the value is 
very limited, 30% → 50% corresponds to the sensitivity loss less than 1%)
