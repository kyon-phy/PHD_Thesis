### PAGE 50
Theory uncertainty estimation
• Theory uncertainties for W+jets and top background are estimated, by following PMG
recommendations.
• Exception: the fraction of b/c jets in W+jets affects our analysis, that is, the fraction of b/c jets could
be the source of the W+jets modeling uncertainties when extrapolating the fit results from b-veto CR
to the b-tag SR/VRs.
→ We decided to apply additional 20% uncertainty for W+jets. (will also try with 10%/30%)
• The uncertainty of “transfer factor (CR→SR)” is estimated.
• Most of uncertainties are cancelled out between SR/VR and CR and found to be less than
20%.
• CR definition is good, and we can use the phase space very similar to SR/VR.
