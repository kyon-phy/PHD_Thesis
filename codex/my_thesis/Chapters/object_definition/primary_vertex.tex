\section{Primary vertex}
\label{sec:ch6_primary_vertex}

A primary vertex (PV) is the reconstructed position of a $pp$ collision.
It is used as the reference for prompt-lepton selection, isolation, pile-up jet rejection, the track contribution to $\met$, and the calculation of track impact parameters, which are used in particle identification, such as the identification of hadronically decaying $\tau$ leptons.
Its position is determined from tracks originating from a common point~\cite{ATLAS2026TrackVertex}.
Several PVs can be reconstructed in an event because of pile-up.
The vertex with the largest sum of squared transverse momenta of its tracks is selected as the PV of the hard scattering.
