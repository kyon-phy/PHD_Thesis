\chapter[Fake-tau estimation]{Fake-$\hadtau$ estimation}
\label{app:ch8_fake_tau}

The fake-$\hadtau$ background from $\znunu$+jets is estimated using the strategy described in Section~\ref{sec:ch8_strategy}.
Its modelling is tested in a $\zll$ validation region (ZVR).

The ZVR requires two signal electrons or two signal muons and exactly one $\hadtau$ candidate, with the dilepton mass restricted to the $Z$-boson mass window.
A $b$-jet veto and looser selections than in the SRs are applied to increase the event yield, and both lepton channels are used.
The quantity $|\vec p_{\mathrm{T}}^{\,\ell\ell}+\mpt|$ is used to emulate the $\met$ in $\znunu$ events by adding the dilepton momentum to $\mpt$.
An additional requirement of $\pT^{\ell\ell}>130~\GeV$ suppresses the $\ttbar$ background and increases the $\zjets$ purity.
The definition is summarised in Table~\ref{tab:app_ch8_zvr_selections}.

A total of 116 data events are selected in the ZVR, with a simulated $\zjets$ purity of approximately $90\%$.
The $\pT^{\hadtau}$ distribution is shown in Figure~\ref{fig:ch8_zvr_pt}.
For the RNN-score study, the $\hadtau$ identification requirement is relaxed to \texttt{Loose}, the $\mT$ requirement is removed, and $\pT^{\hadtau}>20~\GeV$ or $>100~\GeV$ is required.
The remaining selections in Table~\ref{tab:app_ch8_zvr_selections} are then applied.
The RNN-score distributions for different $\pT^{\hadtau}$ thresholds, obtained with the selections above, are shown in Figure~\ref{fig:ch8_zvr_rnn}.
The simulation overestimates the data by a factor of about two at high $\pT^{\hadtau}$.
Both mismodelling of the fake-$\hadtau$ rate and mismodelling of the $\zjets$ kinematics could account for this disagreement.

However, better agreement between data and simulation is observed at low RNN scores in Figure~\ref{fig:ch8_zvr_rnn}(a), where the candidates are less tau-like.
The discrepancy increases towards higher RNN scores and is more pronounced for the higher-$\pT^{\hadtau}$ selection in Figure~\ref{fig:ch8_zvr_rnn}(b).
This pattern suggests that the simulation overestimates the probability for jets to pass the $\hadtau$ identification requirement, making fake-$\hadtau$ rate mismodelling the main source of the disagreement.

This result also motivates the conservative $100\%$ normalisation uncertainty assigned to the $\zjets$ background with a fake $\hadtau$, as described in Section~\ref{subsec:ch9_signal}.
