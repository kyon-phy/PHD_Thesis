\chapter{Zero-b-jet signal-region optimisation}
\label{app:sr0b_optimisation}

The $\met$ threshold in the $\bVetoSR$ regions defined in Section~\ref{sec:ch7_sr0b} is studied to improve the sensitivity.
A higher threshold can suppress both the SM background and the interference, but also drops the signal.
The significance is therefore evaluated as the threshold is varied. For simplicity, only a few leading systematic uncertainties are selected in the study.

The $\met$ threshold is scanned from 200 to $1500~\GeV$ in $100~\GeV$ steps.
The signal benchmarks use the parameter points at $(\MU,\gU,\beta_L^{23})=(1.5~\TeV,0.5,1.8)$ for $\bVetoSR$-$\Res$ and $(3.0~\TeV,2.0,1.8)$ for $\bVetoSR$-$\NonRes$.

The scan result is shown in Figure~\ref{fig:app_sr0b_scan}. For $\bVetoSR$-$\NonRes$, the significance is maximised at $\met>400~\GeV$.
For $\bVetoSR$-$\Res$, the significance is maximised at $\met>600~\GeV$.
The final resonant selection nevertheless retains $\met>200~\GeV$ to keep its kinematic requirements aligned with $\bTagSR$-$\Res$.
