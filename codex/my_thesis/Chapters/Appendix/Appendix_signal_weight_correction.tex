\chapter{Correction of the signal internal event weights}
\label{app:signal_weight_correction}

The signal reweighting used for the right-handed interpretation is introduced in Section~\ref{subsec:signal_samples}.
Its accuracy is tested by comparing reweighted predictions with samples generated independently at selected parameter points.

At some parameter points, the internal event weights alone do not reproduce the cross section or the relative contributions of resonant and non-resonant production in the reference samples.
A correction in either the leading-jet $\pT$ or $m_{b\hadtau}$ alone is insufficient to correct both components across the mass points.
After adjusting the overall normalisation, a bin-by-bin correction is therefore derived from the two-dimensional truth-level distributions of these observables in the reweighted and reference samples.

The corrected predictions are validated using truth-level selections that reproduce the main signal-region requirements.
At each stage of the selection, the corrected and independently generated event yields agree within $10\%$ for both the resonant and non-resonant signal regions.
This difference is smaller than the theory uncertainties assigned to the signal prediction.
