6.5.2 Identification
Hadronic tau candidates are distinguished from quark- and gluon-initiated jets using a separate
RNN discriminant [12, 10]. Its inputs include track momenta and displacements, calorimeter
shower properties and, for three-prong candidates, the displacement of the reconstructed decay
vertex. These variables exploit the narrow shower, low charged multiplicity and displaced
decay of a tau lepton. Nearby activity is included, without a separate isolation working point.
Baseline tau candidates are required to pass Loose RNN identification, and signal candidates
must additionally satisfy Tight. The higher RNN-score threshold for Tight rejects more jets
at the cost of tau efficiency. For reconstructed one-prong (three-prong) candidates, the target
identification efficiencies are 85% (75%) for Loose and 60% (45%) for Tight [10].
Both baseline and signal tau candidates must also pass Loose electron rejection. A separate
RNN uses shower and track information, including the relation between calorimeter energy
and track momentum, to reject electron-like candidates. The tau efficiencies are approximately
95% and 98% for one-prong and three-prong candidates, respectively [10]. Muon rejection
uses the angular separation from reconstructed muons.
6.5.3 Calibration
The tau energy scale is calibrated to the visible decay products [10]. A calorimeter estimate,
corrected for pile-up and detector response, is combined with an estimate from charged-particle
tracks and neutral calorimeter deposits, using weights determined by their expected resolutions.
A boosted regression tree then corrects the combined estimate using shower properties, track
multiplicity and reconstructed decay structure. This calibration is used for the tau kinematics in
the analysis.
Simulated event weights are corrected for differences in tau reconstruction, identification
and electron-rejection efficiencies between data and simulation. For signal candidates pass￾ing Tight tau identification, the electron-rejection scale factor associated with Medium tau
identification is used.
