\section{Primary vertex}
\label{sec:ch6_primary_vertex}

A primary vertex (PV) is the reconstructed position of a $pp$ collision.
It is used as the reference for prompt-lepton selection, isolation, pile-up jet rejection and the track contribution to $\met$.
Its position is determined from tracks originating from a common point.
Several PVs can be reconstructed in an event because of pile-up.
The vertex with the largest sum of squared transverse momenta of its associated tracks is selected as the PV of the hard scattering~\cite{ATLAS2015Vertex}.
