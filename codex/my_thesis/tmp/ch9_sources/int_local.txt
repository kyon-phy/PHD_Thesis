1 Executive Summary84
1.1 Target85
This is a search for leptoquark signals in the𝜏𝜈𝑏 final state, motivated by the𝑅(𝐷 (∗) ) anomalies by the86
BaBar, Belle and LHCb experiments. It is a completely new phase space, previously not explored by87
ATLAS and CMS1. Since the signal strength scales with the 4th power of the coupling constant, the88
sensitivity gain from increased integrated luminosity is rather limited (of course the limit on the signal89
cross-section will be improved by adding more data, though). We agreed with PC/PubCom and relevant90
conveners to use the Run-2 dataset only and aim for an early publication by the end of 2025.91
1.2 Context and Motivation92
Motivate this analysis in 1 paragraph: why is this signature interesting? Which kind of93
models are you probing?94
While the 𝑅(𝐷 (∗) ) anomaly requires both𝑏𝜏 and 𝑐𝜈 couplings of the leptoquark, previous ATLAS95
and CMS searches, focusing on the𝜏𝜏 final state, were sensitive only to the𝑏𝜏 coupling and typically96
fixed the𝑐𝜈 coupling to a small value. This assumption was motivated not by experimental constraints97
but by theoretical considerations: a general UV completion to suppressΔ𝐹=2 transitions. A more98
model-independent approach is crucial, as the UV model may include mechanisms that naturally suppress99
flavor transitions, such as the GIM mechanism in the SM. Our search in the𝜏𝜈𝑏 final state is sensitive to100
both 𝑏𝜏 and 𝑐𝜈 couplings simultaneously, and thus uniquely probes scenarios with larger𝑐𝜈 couplings that101
were previously unexplored.102
