
The missing transverse momentum, denoted by $\met$, is reconstructed from the negative vector sum of the transverse momenta of calibrated physics objects, together with additional contributions from tracks or calorimeter energy deposits depending on the reconstruction algorithm. 
It is sensitive to particles that escape detection, such as neutrinos. 
For the leptoquark search presented in this thesis, large $\met$ is an important feature of the signal region.
