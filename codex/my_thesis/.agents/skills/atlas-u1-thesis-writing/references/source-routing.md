# Source Routing and Evidence Discipline

Use sources according to the claim being written. Do not choose a source only
because it is convenient or written in thesis style. Re-identify a document by
its title and content if synced filenames change.

The user's own INT note and associated leptoquark search paper may supply
information and materials for the manuscript but must never themselves be cited,
including public versions. For material they attribute to other sources, trace
the citations and directly cite the same relevant original public sources after
checking their support, as specified in the parent and global writing skills.
Keep the two intermediate documents' provenance in author-facing notes only.

## Route by claim type

For every information lookup, start with the relevant passages and references
in the user's INT note and associated analysis paper. Use the routes below to
locate original references and supplement what those two documents do not cover.
Other articles and doctoral theses do not override their content in a conflict.

- Analysis model, simulation configuration, selections, categories, regions,
  backgrounds, systematics, fit implementation, validation, and results:
  consult the internal analysis note first.
- Concise analysis motivation and public-facing narrative: consult the ATLAS
  paper-stage document, then check technical details against the internal note.
- Equations, flavour conventions, model assumptions, coupling interpretation,
  and phenomenological scaling: consult the relevant primary theory source.
- Detector, reconstruction, calibration, identification, and statistical
  facts: use official ATLAS or primary technical references when the supplied
  sources do not establish the point.
- Writing voice: infer only from the user's own ATLAS writing and tau-neutrino
  paper, using the direct, physics-driven traits defined in `AGENTS.md`.
- Explanatory depth and order: use only practices demonstrably common to all
  three reference dissertations.

## Conflict procedure

1. Check document version, dataset, definitions, flavour basis, coupling
   assumptions, simulation setup, selections, and statistical model.
2. Do not mix equations, conventions, or numerical values from incompatible
   versions or scenarios.
3. When another article or doctoral thesis conflicts with the user's INT note
   or associated analysis paper, give the latter two priority. Between those
   two, retain the established INT-note priority for analysis definitions and
   implementation. Do not silently substitute another study's configuration.
4. Record unresolved discrepancies and their consequences in author-facing
   notes. Check original public references for the relevant supporting claim,
   preserving the source hierarchy and the ban on citing the two documents.
5. Ask the user only when the unresolved choice materially changes the thesis
   and cannot be inferred safely from the established analysis convention.

## Evidence and originality

- Separate what a source states from a derivation, an inference, and a writing
  recommendation.
- Support equations, numerical values, working points, and result statements
  with the appropriate source; never fabricate a citation.
- Treat sources as evidence, not prose templates. Extract the physical point,
  understand its role in the argument, and reconstruct the explanation in the
  user's voice.
- Preserve necessary definitions, notation, equations, numbers, and
  conventions, but not source phrasing.
